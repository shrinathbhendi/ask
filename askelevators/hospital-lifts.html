Hospital & Stretcher Elevators

ASK Elevators manufactures high-capacity hospital and stretcher elevators built to satisfy the vertical transportation standards of hospitals, trauma centers, nursing homes, and clinics. Designed with deep, wide cabins that comfortably accommodate hospital beds, stretchers, wheel chairs, medical equipment, and accompanying healthcare staff.

Equipped with micro-levelling control to ensure zero floor bumps during patient transfer, emergency key-switch priority recall, and easy-to-sanitize stainless steel interiors.

Key Features & Healthcare Specs

  • Smooth Jerk-Free Levelling (±2 mm)
  • Deep Cabins for Stretchers & Beds
  • Emergency Priority Recall Switch
  • Antimicrobial SS 304 Interiors
