About ASK Elevators

Our Story

Vertical Transportation Designed Around Your Needs

Established in 2012 in Pune, Maharashtra, ASK Elevators is a trusted manufacturer of Passenger Elevators, Hospital Elevators, and other lift solutions.

Our spacious infrastructure is equipped with advanced machinery, and our processes are systematically divided into departments handled by experienced managers.

Over the years, we have built a reputation for excellence, reliability, and unparalleled customer service. From conceptual design to precise installation and rigorous maintenance, our dedicated team ensures every project meets the highest standards of quality and safety.
