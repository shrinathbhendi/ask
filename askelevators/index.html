About ASK Elevators

Vertical Transportation Designed Around Your Needs

Established in 2012 in Pune, Maharashtra, ASK Elevators is a trusted manufacturer of Passenger Elevators, Hospital Elevators, and other lift solutions.

Our spacious infrastructure is equipped with advanced machinery, and our processes are systematically divided into departments handled by experienced managers. We are dedicated to providing reliable vertical mobility tailored to your requirements.
